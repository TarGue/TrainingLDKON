## 1. Wikidata
1. Please open Wikidata: [https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Main_Page
](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Main_Page
)
2. Go to the searchbar and type in what you like "..." e.g. apple, caffein. 

- Be aware that you will find a lot of results. Wikidata is a huge resource including publications as well.

## 2. Ontology Looklup Services

You can also check out ontology lookup services which store relevant terminology in the life science domain.

1. Visit one of the following Look Up Services  
- [Ontology LookUp Service by EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/)
- [Ontobee](https://ontobee.org/)
- [Bioportal](https://bioportal.bioontology.org/)

2. Search for a Fruit, Chemical or Organism of your favour.
